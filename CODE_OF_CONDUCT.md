# Code of conduct

SecondHand exists to make asking for help a little easier and a lot more dignified. We expect the same respect inside the project.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in SecondHand to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or immigration status.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Remember who SecondHand is for. Talk about people who receive assistance with respect, never as stereotypes or jokes.
- Protect privacy. Never share anyone's personal details, including applicants', without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Publishing someone's private information
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributions that break this code, and may ban people who repeatedly or seriously break it.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability** works for conduct reports too), or message a maintainer directly. Reports are kept confidential.
