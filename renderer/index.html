    Use Chrome 116 or newer. Chrome requires you to load this early version once. SecondHand prepares the files and connects them to the app automatically.

    1. 1

      Prepare your extension

      Save the included extension to a permanent folder on this computer. We’ll register its local connection and open the folder for you.

      Files ready on your computer

      Keep this folder in place, and keep Developer mode on in Chrome. Chrome loads the extension from here. After a SecondHand update, the app refreshes these files and the extension reloads itself.

    2. 2

      Load it in Chrome

      Open Chrome and enter chrome://extensions in the address bar. Turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, then select the folder from step 1.

      Use Copy folder path above to find the folder in Chrome’s chooser.

      You do this step in Chrome. Preparing the files does not install the extension automatically.

    3. 3

      Autofill Iowa’s form

      Keep this app open and unlocked, then open Iowa’s portal in Chrome and click Autofill on the page. On supported Iowa applicant, home-address, complete Tell Us More, screening, and financial record pages, SecondHand can choose Save and Continue, which sends that page’s answers to Iowa. It selects the first suggested home address; review that choice and every answer before submitting. Missing questions, summaries, Add Another, household relationships, and legal or representative questions still need you.

      When SecondHand is unlocked, Chrome can fill your saved answers without another pop-up, on every site SecondHand is on. That includes your Social Security number, birth date, income, benefits, and citizenship and disability answers. It also continues through supported Iowa applicant, home-address, complete Tell Us More, screening, and financial record pages. It shares one person’s saved record at a time; you choose if several match. On other sites, optional Fill and continue uses saved answers only and may click an ordinary Next or Continue after supported questions are complete. It stops for missing answers, errors, and protected steps. Review every answer and the first suggested home address before submitting. Lock SecondHand to stop.

        Privacy & backups

        Stored on this computer

        SecondHand keeps your profile and application records in an encrypted file on this computer. There is no SecondHand cloud account or online profile.

        When you share information

        When you approve a fill, the requested details are sent locally to the extension and entered into Iowa’s portal or another site you have approved. That site can receive or save information as you type or fill it, even before you submit.

        Your government account and information you submit are handled by Iowa’s services.

        While SecondHand is unlocked

        SecondHand needs access to your details to display and fill them. Lock SecondHand when you step away or share this computer.

        Keep your operating system and browser up to date. Local storage does not protect against someone who controls your unlocked computer.

        Use your fingerprint instead of typing your password. Touch ID stays on until you turn it off, and anyone who can use this Mac account’s Touch ID or login password could unlock SecondHand too.

        How autofill works

        Autofill asks the first time. Choose Always allow to skip the pop-up while SecondHand is unlocked, on every site SecondHand is on. That includes your Social Security number, birth date, income, benefits, and citizenship and disability answers. When another site’s pop-up asks about those details, Always allow on this site skips it there alone. Only the saved answers a page needs leave SecondHand.

        You finish the important steps

        Review every answer. Signatures, uploads, account access, verification, and final submission stay with you.

        What it can fill today

        On Iowa’s portal, the verified first applicant page includes names and suffix, maiden name, explicit program and applicant choices, home and mailing addresses, phone numbers, and best contact time. Once that page is complete, it chooses Save and Continue.

        On Household Application Information, it answers Yes to whether anyone is applying when you saved Yes to requesting a program.

        On the Tell Us More page, it fills your date of birth and the answers you saved under Iowa’s questions about you. A question you left Not answered is filled from a household answer only when that answer settles it, such as nobody in your household having a disability.

        On the Tell Us More page, Autofill may fill the Social Security number saved under About you, once the page records Yes to having one. Once the page also records that your card has a different name, it may fill that name from More SNAP information. SecondHand can’t tell whether Iowa kept the number, so check that number in Iowa’s form before submitting.

        On the complete captured Tell Us More layout, Save and Continue can run only after all visible questions are answered, the submitted SSN matches when present, and no unknown questions, errors, or dialogs remain. The older birth-date-only layout still needs manual Continue.

        On the verified home-address page, it selects Iowa’s first suggested home address and saves that step. Check that this address is yours before you submit.

        Information-only screens are continued for you. On Iowa’s Job Information, Income Information, Expenses Information and Property Information pages, it answers household questions from More SNAP information, and Laya stays off. Captured emergency and background pages also use explicit saved answers.

        Supported financial forms can use one person’s saved job, pension or Social Security income, rent responsibility, utility answers, or Cash/Uncashed Check asset. If several records match, choose one in the app. These and the captured screening pages can continue only after all visible questions are supported and complete; no current amount or owner is inferred.

        Summaries, Add Another, household relationships, Other Information/legal/representative questions, and final review remain manual. Other Iowa pages may fill matching saved answers, but you continue them yourself.

        On the sites you turned on, listed under All websites and Other sites you trust on the Chrome extension page, it fills saved answers that match a question. Matched answers for your Social Security number, birth date, income and other sensitive details wait in SecondHand’s side panel in Chrome until you click Fill sensitive details and allow them, unless Always allow covers that site.

        Custom answers in My information reuse the answer you entered when a whole question label or explicit alias matches uniquely. Custom values are sensitive, use the same site permissions and approvals, and are never sent to Laya.

        On other sites, choose the optional Fill and continue mode to use saved answers only and move through ordinary Next or Continue buttons. It stops for missing answers, errors, unknown controls, and protected steps. Review, consent, signatures, payments, and final submission remain yours; choosing this mode does not authorize them.

        With ordinary Autofill, when Laya is ready, it answers questions no saved answer matches, on those sites and on Iowa pages SecondHand doesn’t know, only when it is sure. They are marked suggested by Laya, to check. On those sites only, when Laya isn’t ready or can’t take any of the open questions, Chrome’s built-in AI on this computer may suggest answers instead, also marked to check.

        Chrome’s AI never fills Iowa’s form.

        The page widget and sidebar show what still needs you. Iowa can receive answers during filling and automatic Save and Continue. Review all answers before signing or submitting yourself. A complete live submission has not been validated.
