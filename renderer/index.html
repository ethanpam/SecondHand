A HAND WITH THE PAPERWORK

Connect Chrome

One local setup. Then your confirmed details are ready when you need them.

Needs setup

Set up your Chrome companion

Use Chrome 116 or newer. Chrome requires you to load this early version once. SecondHand prepares the files and connects them to your vault automatically.

  1. 1

    Prepare your extension

    Save the included extension to a permanent folder on this computer. We’ll register its local connection and open the folder for you.

    Files ready on your computer

    Keep this folder in place. Chrome loads the extension from here.

  2. 2

    Load it in Chrome

    Open Chrome and enter chrome://extensions in the address bar. Turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, then select the folder from step 1.

    Use Copy folder path above to find the folder in Chrome’s chooser.

    You do this step in Chrome. Preparing the files does not install the extension automatically.

  3. 3

    Autofill Iowa’s form

    Keep this app open and unlocked, then open Iowa’s portal in Chrome and click Autofill on the page. You review the answers and click Iowa’s Continue yourself.

    When SecondHand is unlocked, the extension fills your saved answers on Iowa’s supported page without a pop-up. Lock SecondHand to stop.

    The portal button opens your default browser. If that isn’t Chrome, copy the portal address into Chrome.

Advanced: use a different extension ID

Only needed for a custom build. The included extension is connected automatically in step 1. A custom ID replaces that connection.

You’re in control.

Autofill asks the first time. Choose Always allow to skip the pop-up while SecondHand is unlocked. Only saved answers for Iowa’s supported page leave your vault.

You finish the important steps

Review every answer. Signatures, uploads, account access, verification, and final submission stay with you.

AFTER AN APP UPDATE

Prepare the extension again, then click its reload icon on Chrome’s extensions page. Your local profile stays in your vault.

YOUR INFORMATION BELONGS TO YOU

Privacy & backups

A local vault. A clear picture of where your information goes.

On your device, by default.

SecondHand keeps your profile and application records in an encrypted file on this computer. There is no SecondHand cloud account or online profile.

When you share information

When you approve a fill, the requested details are sent locally to the extension and entered into Iowa’s portal. The portal can receive or save information as you type or fill it, even before you submit.

Your government account and information you submit are handled by Iowa’s services.

While your vault is unlocked

SecondHand needs access to your details to display and fill them. Lock your vault when you step away or share this computer.

Keep your operating system and browser up to date. Local storage does not protect against someone who controls your unlocked computer.

Give your information a backup

Export an encrypted copy of your vault to a location you choose.

You’ll need your vault passphrase to restore it. Choose a removable drive or another location you trust. A folder synced by other software may upload your backup.

Need to restore a backup?

Lock your vault, then choose “Restore an encrypted backup” on the unlock screen. Restoring replaces the current local vault.

SecondHand is an independent application assistant. It does not decide eligibility or represent Iowa HHS.
