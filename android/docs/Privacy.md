# Privacy and data handling

Second Hand is an independent prototype. It has no app account, analytics, backend, or cross-device synchronization. Only opening and using Iowa's website requires a network connection.

## On-device records

- Profile, household notes, renewal history, and document metadata are serialized into an AES-256-GCM encrypted vault.
- Imported documents are encrypted separately, with their unique IDs bound into authenticated ciphertext. Files are capped at 20 MB each.
- The non-exportable key is generated by Android Keystore. Its hardware protection depends on the device. Key use requires the device to be unlocked; the app separately requires strong biometrics or device credentials to unlock its UI.
- Vault files and reminder schedule metadata live under `noBackupFilesDir`. App backup and device transfer are disabled. Losing the device/key or uninstalling can make information unrecoverable.
- Authentication gates access. Backgrounding locks the app and revokes its short-lived sharing grant. Sensitive screens use Android's secure-window flag to block ordinary screenshots and recent-app previews.
- Document previews remain inside the app. Any necessary temporary decrypted representation is removed after preview; the encrypted original remains until deleted.

## Reminders

Reminders use dates the applicant enters from their notice. The app does not assume all SNAP renewals occur yearly. Notifications contain generic wording, with no benefit amounts, names, case numbers, or documents. Android may delay inexact reminders because of battery restrictions; check the actual notice independently.

## Website assistance

An explicit, ten-minute, memory-only grant permits the assistant to use name, email, explicit home/mobile phone numbers, home address, monthly income, and monthly housing cost. Household members, generic phone, notes, documents, signatures, passwords, SSNs, and birth dates are not released by autofill. The snapshot is revoked on lock or Stop and is never a background renewal authorization.

The assistant runs bundled code in an isolated WebView JavaScript world. Exact origin, main-frame, document, request, URL, expiry, and approval checks separate it from website scripts. Saved answers are supplied only for a native-user-authorized operation. The app does not expose `addJavascriptInterface` to webpages or fall back to page-world privileged injection.

Once an answer is filled into Iowa's form, it is available to that website just as if typed. Stopping assistance cannot retract data already delivered to the site. Page changes, expiry, and cancellation invalidate pending actions. Signature and submission approval remain separate user actions; an attempted click is never proof of filing.

The in-app browser uses a separate WebView session within this app. Ending it clears browser state best-effort; it does not change the user's Chrome or other browser profile. Authentication and portal compatibility must be tested on actual devices. Unsupported WebView providers and unrecognized pages offer manual use.

## Limits

Android's application sandbox and Keystore do not protect against every compromised-device scenario, and managed-language memory cannot guarantee immediate zeroization. Do not treat this prototype as a production benefits service. Live submission, authenticated renewal, and full device behavior remain unverified. See [validation](Validation.md).
